# Phonetic-Speller
# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Phonetic Speller is designed to work without collecting personal data.

## Data Collection

Phonetic Speller does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

The letters you select in the app are used only to display or pronounce NATO phonetic alphabet replacements, such as "A for Alfa". This text stays on your device and is not sent to a server by the app.

## Speech

When you use the voice feature, Phonetic Speller uses Apple's system speech synthesis APIs to speak the selected letters. The app does not record your voice and does not create audio files.

## Network Access

Phonetic Speller does not require an internet connection for its core features.

## Analytics and Tracking

Phonetic Speller does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection tools.

## Children's Privacy

Phonetic Speller does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's behavior changes. Any updates should describe the new behavior clearly.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the support channel provided with the app listing.
